About PharmaGuard

In today’s healthcare systems, patients are often prescribed multiple medications to treat complex or coexisting medical conditions. While essential for managing health, this multi-drug approach significantly increases the risk of Drug-to-Drug Interactions (DDIs) and unwanted side effects.

These interactions can reduce drug efficacy, cause allergic responses, or lead to severe, even life-threatening complications. Without proper tools, healthcare providers and patients face challenges in identifying these risks in real-time.

PharmaGuard solves this problem with an AI-powered platform that allows users to input drugs and receive immediate insights about:

  • Potential drug-to-drug interactions
  • Associated side effects with severity levels
  • Relevant clinical insights for safe usage

Whether used in clinics or at home, PharmaGuard helps reduce medication risks and ensures confident, informed health decisions. Our goal is to support informed decision-making, reduce avoidable medication errors, and promote patient safety.
